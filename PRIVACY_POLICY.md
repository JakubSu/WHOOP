# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This application uses the WHOOP API to access fitness and wellness data authorized by the user.

Data accessed may include sleep, recovery, workout, body measurement, and profile information.

Data is used solely to provide application features and insights requested by the user.

We do not sell user data or use it for advertising.

Data may be stored securely to support application functionality.

Users may revoke WHOOP access at any time and may request deletion of their data by contacting kuba.suran.15@gmail.com.

We take reasonable measures to protect user data from unauthorized access.
