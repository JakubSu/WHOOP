01 / Features

What is available
and what is next.

Current features

Available
  • 01
    Workout generation and weekly planning

    Request workouts for specific dates, choose the exercises, duration, and structure, then see every session across your weekly plan.

  • 02
    Desktop and mobile web interface

    Use the same training workspace across desktop and mobile browsers, wherever you plan or train.

  • 03
    Readiness context from WHOOP

    View Sleep, Recovery, and Strain data alongside your training workspace so every session starts with the right context.

  • 04
    AI Coach workout proposals

    Ask for a workout or a change, then review the Coach’s proposal before accepting or rejecting it.

02 / Why I built it

01

Because “close enough”
isn’t the workout I asked for.

When I was using WHOOP Strength Trainer, I asked the coach to create workouts with exercises that were not in its library. It replaced them with unrelated exercises instead.

It also did not let me plan workouts in the future. I wanted a coach that could follow a specific request and maintain a plan beyond today.

AI Coach is my project to build that workflow.
